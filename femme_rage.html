sound art, performance

femme rage

Femme rage is a collection of sounds made from recordings of my voice. I manipulated my silent and loud screams and shouts digitally, mixed them, creating a tense and mysterious atmosphere for repressed emotions to find catharsis.
I performed my electroacoustic improvisational piece in live, accompanied with Charlene Galea's improvised movements.
